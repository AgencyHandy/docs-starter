**Chats** is where you and your team message clients in real time. A client conversation starts with one client, the primary client. You can add more people from the client's side, such as a colleague or a manager, so everyone works in the same thread.

Open **Chats** from the **Help Desk** section of the left sidebar.

## Client contacts

Open a client conversation and its **Details** panel. The **Client contacts** section lists everyone on the client side of the thread:

- The primary client, marked **Primary**.
- Each contact you added, with their email address.

![Client contacts section in the Details panel of a chat](/images/chats/client-contacts-panel.png)

### Add contacts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    In **Client contacts**, click **Add contact**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the people to add">
    Search by name or email. The list shows people from the primary client's company first, under **From** and the company name. Other clients of your workspace follow under **Other clients**. People already on the thread don't appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add them">
    Select one or more people and click **Add**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![Add contact dialog with people from the client's company listed first](/images/chats/add-contact-dialog.png)

### Remove a contact

Click the **×** next to a contact and confirm. The contact loses access to the conversation right away. You can't remove the primary client.

### Limits

- A conversation can have up to 20 contacts.
- A contact must already be a client in the same workspace. To add someone new, first create them as a client in **Clients**.

## Who can manage contacts

| Role | Can add and remove contacts |
|---|---|
| Workspace admins | Yes |
| Team members assigned to the conversation | Yes |
| Other team members | No |
| Clients | No |

## How contacts take part

- **Email notifications.** Every contact gets each new message by email.
- **Reply by email or in the portal.** A contact can answer the email or open the chat in their client portal. An email reply appears in the same conversation, posted as that contact.
- **Unknown senders are ignored.** Replies from an email address that isn't on the thread are not posted.
- **Shared unread count.** Everyone on the client side of the thread shares one unread count.

## Privacy

- A client only sees the conversations they're on. Adding someone to one conversation doesn't give them access to the client's other conversations.
- Clients don't see each other's email addresses. In the portal, other contacts appear only by name and avatar.
